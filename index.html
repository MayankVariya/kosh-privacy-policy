Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 • Application: Kosh: Personal Financial Vault

1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

Kosh ("we", "our", or "the app") is engineered from the ground up as an offline-first, sovereign personal financial ledger and vault. We hold a fundamental conviction that your personal wealth and ledger data belong exclusively to you.

Unlike conventional financial management and banking applications, Kosh operates without centralized servers, remote databases, or mandatory user accounts. Your transactions, accounts, and financial records reside strictly on your local device hardware.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Financial Data Collection: We do not track, collect, inspect, or monetize your transaction amounts, notes, bank balances, or investment holdings.
  • No Personal Identity Tracking: We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, email address, or device hardware IDs.
  • Zero Advertising Frameworks: Kosh contains zero third-party advertising SDKs (no Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or programmatic ad bidders).
  • Zero Telemetry & Tracking SDKs: Kosh contains zero behavioral analytics or user tracking frameworks (no Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel, or Crashlytics).
  • No SMS or Bank Credential Scraping: Kosh does not read your device SMS inbox, email messages, or bank credentials.

3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

All financial records entered into Kosh are stored inside an isolated SQLite database on your device's private application storage sandbox:

  • Account names, account categories (Cash, Bank, Investment), and balances
  • Transaction history (dates, amounts, categories, self/family attribution, notes)
  • Custom user categories and frozen fund commitments
  • Security settings (biometric lock toggle)

This data is never transmitted to the developer, and cannot be accessed by other applications on your device.

4. Biometric Security & Authentication

Kosh offers an optional biometric lock feature (Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or Device PIN):

  • Biometric verification is handled entirely by your mobile device operating system via the Android Biometric API.
  • Kosh never accesses or stores your raw biometric data or fingerprints. Verification occurs inside your phone's hardware security enclave (Trusted Execution Environment / TEE).
  • The application receives only a cryptographic success or failure callback from the operating system to grant entry.

5. Optional Google Drive & Sheets Backup (User-Initiated)

If you choose to sync your transactions to a personal Google Spreadsheet, Kosh connects directly from your device to Google APIs over HTTPS via OAuth 2.0:

  • Direct Device-to-Cloud Connection: The sync connection is established directly between your phone and your private Google Drive account.
  • No Intermediary Servers: The developer operates no proxy servers, backend databases, or logging mechanisms. We never see, store, or receive your Google credentials, OAuth tokens, or spreadsheets.
  • Revocable Access: You can disconnect Google Sign-In or revoke access at any time from within the app settings or via your Google Account Permissions portal.

7. Data Deletion & Total User Sovereignty

You retain 100% control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Data Purge: You can erase all transaction records at any time from Settings → Clear All Transactions.
  • Complete App Wipe: Clearing app storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app permanently and irreversibly deletes the local SQLite database and all associated keys.

8. Children's Privacy

Kosh is a personal wealth management tool intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit data from children.
